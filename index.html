FAQ

Questions before you join

What's included in a membership?

There are three tiers. All Particles ($20/month) has 900+ particle effects and 1GB+ of new sprites. Scenebuilds + Particles ($25/month) adds 30+ scenebuilds and 1GB+ of models. The full library ($30/month) adds 600+ animations for HWM characters, HL2 rigs and cameras. Each tier includes everything in the tiers before it. The side-by-side breakdown is on the Patreon page.

How do the downloads work?

Everything is delivered through Patreon. The assets are consolidated in usermod folders with no outside model usage, with clear folder names, expanded docs and PNG previews, so you can find an effect fast. Single packs can also be bought one by one in the Patreon shop.

Can I try anything before I join?

Yes. Free trial versions of several packs, including Enhanced Fire, the Explosions MEGA-PACK and both Skibidi packs, are on the Steam Workshop, along with other free items. See the Workshop page. The tutorials and the HoovyTools add-ons are free too.

Do I have to credit HoovyTube?

Several of the free Workshop packs say it on their Steam pages: credit is not required, but appreciated. If you make something with the library, I'd genuinely like to see it: send it over.

Can I use the assets in my own videos?

That's what they're for. Animators across the community build with them, and a few of them are in the clip wall above.

Who handles billing, and can I cancel?

Patreon handles the membership and the billing. Billing and cancelling are both done from your Patreon account.

Are the Blender add-ons really free?

Yes, HoovyTools is free for everyone. Session import runs on top of SourceIO, so install that first. Particle Import is standalone and needs nothing but Blender 4.2 or newer. Full steps are on the HoovyTools page.

Do you teach SFM & Blender?

Yes, and it's free: 15 tutorials on the Learn page, sorted Easy, Medium and Hard so you can jump in at your level. Everything in the paid library is something you can learn to build yourself.
