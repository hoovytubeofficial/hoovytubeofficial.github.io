HoovyTools - free Blender add-ons

Bring SFM into Blender, automatically

Point it at a session file and it rebuilds that session inside Blender in one pass - shape keys, cameras, sounds and animation, auto-timed to the frame. Built between 2023 and 2026, on top of SourceIO with RedEye's blessing.

Session import v1.3.1 Particle import v0.23.0 Blender → SFM · in progress
  • Resolves every asset through your own usermod and mounted game folders - nothing renamed, nothing re-pathed.
  • Reads .pcf, .vtf and .vmt directly. No converters, no VTFEdit, no extra add-ons.
  • Particle systems come in as live, editable geometry nodes - not baked meshes - so you can keep directing them in Blender.
  • Tested against hundreds of custom particle systems, plus TF2 and SFM stock effects.
