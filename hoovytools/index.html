SFM → Blender Session Importer
Download the .zip above (don't unzip it). In Blender: Edit → Preferences → Add-ons → Install…, pick the zip, then tick the checkbox to enable HoovyTools. It'll appear in the sidebar (press N in the 3D viewport).
Point it at your SFM session and it reads the .dmx, then pulls your models, animation, and now audio onto the Blender timeline — auto-timed to the frame and FPS-aware (24 → 30). No manual re-timing.
Session Sounds Auto-Importer — pulls every voice line from your SFM session onto the Blender timeline, on the frame. Now faster (up to 100× on big sessions).
