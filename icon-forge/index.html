Tips

  • Plain black icons (SVG or PNG) work best. Thin line icons look chunkier with a little Grow.
  • Opaque images (JPG, screenshots) are traced against their own background colour, so a white logo on orange or a dark one on blue works too. Lower Threshold if faint parts go missing, raise it if background bits sneak in.
  • Holes stay see-through like the house door and the Discord eyes (the dark crescent at the top is the 3D edge). Plug with outline colour gives a solid slate centre; Only plug holes smaller than fills small detail holes but keeps big ones open.
  • Colour logos with inner parts in another colour (line work, or a white play triangle on a red logo): tick Keep inner details to cut them out as slate. The card says when an image has such details.
  • Same engine in code: import { forgeIcon } from '/shared/icon-forge.js'. Batch: node scripts/forge-icons.mjs.
