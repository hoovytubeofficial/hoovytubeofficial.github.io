Answers

Tutorial FAQ

Are the tutorials really free?

Yes. You don't have to pay a cent: every skill used to build the library is walked through on YouTube, with no paywall. You can rebuild all of it yourself.

Where should I start?

If you've never opened SFM, start with Easy. Comfortable with the basics? Medium levels up your scenes, and Hard goes deep into the techniques behind the packs.

Can I get the finished assets instead?

Yes. The library is on Patreon in three tiers (memberships start at $20/month), single packs are in the Patreon shop, and free items are on the Steam Workshop.

Is there anything for Blender?

Yes: HoovyTools, two free Blender add-ons. One imports a whole SFM session; the other rebuilds .pcf particle effects as editable geometry nodes.
