Good to know

Credits, tips and sources

Straight from the item pages on Steam.

  • Credit not required, but appreciated

    That's what the Smoke, Candle, Airstrike Explosion and Wave pages say.

  • No DLC needed

    Every VTF and VMT in Candle Particles is custom-made, so no DLC is needed. The Airstrike Explosion page also says no DLC is required.

  • Make the Electric Arc glow

    Add $phongboost above 1 and raise the camera's bloom scale.

  • Why Candle Particles is 13 MB

    The size comes from 30+ FPS animated smoke and candle-burn sprites in custom VTFs.

  • Quarry Terrain Pack sources

    The .glb sources are credited to xtremederpie and Per's Scan Collection. HoovyTube ported them and made the textures and bumpmaps, and the artwork is by NukeWizard.

  • Medieval Pack sources

    The .glb sources are credited to Libor Telupil, timowes, D.Milekhin, Andy Woodhead and Abandoned Scans (Sketchfab). HoovyTube ported the mdl, vtf and vmt files and the bumpmaps.
