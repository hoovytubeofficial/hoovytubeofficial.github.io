Before you write

Quick answers

The questions that come up most.

Can I use the effects in my own videos?

That's what they're for. Animators across the community build with them. If you make something with the library, I'd genuinely like to see it: send it over.

Where do I get the full asset library?

The library, 900+ particle effects, 600+ animations and 30+ scenebuilds, is on Patreon in three tiers; memberships start at $20/month. A good chunk is free on the Steam Workshop, and single packs are in the Patreon shop.

Do I need anything else to run the add-ons?

Session import runs on top of SourceIO, so install that first. Particle Import is standalone and needs nothing but Blender 4.2 or newer. Full steps are on the HoovyTools page.

Do you teach SFM and Blender?

Yep, and it's all free. The tutorials live on the Learn page, sorted Easy, Medium and Hard so you can jump in at your level.

How do I support you and stay in touch?

Join the Patreon for the full asset library, subscribe on YouTube, or join the newsletter for new videos, drops and updates.
